Baseball Short Draft Uploader

A personal desktop tool that sends baseball commentary short videos, made by the channel owner, to the owner's own TikTok account as drafts (inbox). The owner reviews each draft and posts it manually in the TikTok app. The tool never posts publicly by itself.

自分で制作した野球解説の短い動画を、自分の TikTok アカウントの下書き（受信箱）に送るための個人用デスクトップツールです。公開の投稿は、本人が TikTok アプリで確認してから手動で行います。
